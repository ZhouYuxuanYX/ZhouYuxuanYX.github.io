Deployed at Scale

ECHO — Efficient Causal Self-Recursion Beyond Auxiliary Multi-Token Prediction (code)
A multi-token prediction paradigm in which the backbone's own final layer predicts every horizon. In pretraining it gains on reasoning and code while holding on the other task classes, and widens its lead across all classes after supervised finetuning; the same shared layer also lets the pretrained model draft for itself. Validated on a production model at scale.
MultiMax — sparse yet diverse output distributions (ICML 2024)
A learnable alternative to SoftMax that suppresses the long tail of implausible tokens while preserving the diversity of plausible ones. Validated on a production model at scale, at negligible cost: merely around 1% training slowdown, no extra memory, and a pretraining-loss gain above 0.01.
